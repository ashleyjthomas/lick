Four sample scenes showing what fresh art for the 3 × 2 × 2 design could look like. All figures are inline SVG (small, scales cleanly, easy to recolor or relabel later). The same building blocks compose all 12 cells.

Notes

  • One protagonist sprite works for all 12 scenes — only her name in the narration changes per trial. (Optional: vary hair color/clothing color per protagonist if you'd rather have visually distinct girls.)
  • Mom vs Teacher are visually distinct: different hair (mom = down hair, teacher = bun + glasses) and clothing color (mom = pink, teacher = purple).
  • Kazoo vs Drumstick + Drum distinguishes the action type. Wind-swoosh lines + tipped kazoo on the ground signals the Prosocial setup.
  • Protagonist absence shown as a dashed-footprint trail going off-screen with a tiny caption "(name is gone)".
  • Style: deliberately spare, friendly, no fine detail — keeps cognitive load low for 6–8 year olds and avoids reading the children as a specific race/ethnicity.
