# LICK-4 — Ms. Fox's lines

The six clips the fox speaks in her own voice. These were recorded without
ever being written down; this is a reconstruction from what each clip does in
the code, the fragments quoted in its comments, and the lengths of the
original recordings. Treat it as a draft to perform, not a transcript.

Record as video with the Zoom fox avatar. One continuous take, **three seconds
of silence between clips** — the splitter uses those gaps.

Target lengths are the originals, so the pacing matches the screens they play
on. Being a second or two over is fine; the buttons wait for you to finish.

---

### 1. `fox_intro.mp3` — 12.7s
Plays on the welcome screen, fox centred and large. First thing the child hears.

> Hi! I'm Ms. Fox. Thank you for coming to play with me today.
> We're going to watch some short videos together, and after each one
> I'll ask you a few questions. There are no right or wrong answers —
> I just want to know what *you* think.

---

### 2. `fox_pie_intro.mp3` — 12.4s
Introduces the reward. The pie tin is spotlighted beside her, then settles into
the corner. It starts empty and gains one slice per trial.

> Oh — and guess what? Every time we finish a video and you answer my
> questions, we get a piece of pie! See the pie tin down here? It's empty
> right now. If we fill it all the way up with six pieces, we get a whole
> pie. Let's see if we can do it!

---

### 3. `fox_button_training.mp3` — 14.2s
Two demo buttons are on screen beside her: orange "Watch again" with a replay
arrow, green "Next" with a play arrow. Neither is clickable here.

> Before we start, let me show you two buttons. If you want to see a video
> again, press the orange one that says "Watch again" — you can watch as
> many times as you like. When you're finished watching and you're ready
> for my questions, press the green one that says "Next". Ready?

---

### 4. `fox_pie_slice_first.mp3` — 5.3s
After the first trial only. The first slice appears as you speak.

> Thank you for telling me! Look — we got a piece of pie!

---

### 5. `fox_pie_slice_more.mp3` — 5.5s
After trials 2, 4 and 6. Keep it brief; they hear it three times.

> Thanks for telling me! That's another piece of pie for us.

---

### 6. `fox_pie_slice_more_yum.mp3` — 9.7s
After trials 3 and 5 — the same moment as clip 5, alternated so it doesn't
get repetitive. This is the one with room for a bit more warmth.

> Nice work! That's another piece of pie. Mmm, yum — I love pie.
> Let's see the next one!
