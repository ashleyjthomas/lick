# LICK-4 narration --- single-take recording sheet

## How to record

1.  Record one continuous file. Save as **`audio/full_recording.wav`** (or `.m4a` / `.mp3` --- I'll convert). Save somewhere you can find it; ping me when it's done.
2.  Before each numbered clip below, **count silently for 3 seconds** (this gives me the gap I need to auto-split).
3.  Read each clip naturally at your normal pace.
4.  If you flub a line, just leave another **3-second silence**, then re-read the line. I'll keep the latter take. (Or tell me which clip you re-did.)
5.  There's no need to say the clip number out loud --- the silences are the splitter.

Total: **24 clips**, probably \~5--7 minutes of recording.
